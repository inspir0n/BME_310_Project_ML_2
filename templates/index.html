What this is

The problem, the data, and what was actually built.

The NIH ChestX-ray14 dataset contains 112,120 frontal chest radiographs from 30,805 patients, each labelled for up to 14 pathologies by mining the text of radiology reports. This project reproduces a 2023 paper's approach to classifying them: an ImageNet-pretrained EfficientNet-B1 backbone, fine-tuned with a small classifier head, one independent sigmoid output per disease.

It's a course project, built and debugged from the ground up — including a genuine reproducibility bug found in a public pretrained checkpoint along the way. The methodology page covers what deviates from the paper and why, and where the paper itself turned out to have errors.

This model is a student replication project, not a medical device. The live demo is for exploring how the model behaves — not for diagnosis, and not validated for clinical use.
